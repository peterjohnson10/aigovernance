2. What we collect, and why

The Readiness Scan

The diagnostic itself runs entirely in your browser. Your answers, your score, and your report are never transmitted anywhere unless you take one of the two optional actions below.

Anonymous benchmark (optional)If you opt in, we receive your size band, sector, country, and domain scores. No name, email, company, or IP address is included. Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by the checkbox.
Contact details (optional)If you enter your name, email, or company, that information is sent to us so we can respond to you. Legal basis: consent, given by voluntarily entering the fields and submitting the form.

The Playbook waitlist

If you join the waitlist, we collect your name, email, company, and stated interest, so we can notify you when the relevant edition is available. Legal basis: consent, and preparatory steps under Art. 6(1)(b) taken at your request.

Purchasing the Playbook

When the Core Edition and Sector Editions become available for purchase, payment will be processed by Stripe. We do not receive or store your card details; Stripe processes them directly under its own privacy policy and PCI-DSS obligations. We receive confirmation of your purchase, your email address, and transaction metadata necessary to deliver the product and meet our accounting and tax obligations. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) and legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).

We do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts of any kind on this site. Nothing about your visit is logged beyond what your browser and our hosting provider retain for basic security purposes, described below.

LinkedIn Newsletter

This site links to our newsletter on LinkedIn. If you subscribe there, that relationship exists entirely on LinkedIn's platform, under LinkedIn's own privacy policy. We do not receive, see, or store your email address or any other personal data as a result, and we cannot access a list of subscribers. LinkedIn Corporation is the data controller for that subscription, not us.

Contact and waitlist submissions are kept until you ask us to delete them, or until the purpose they were collected for is fulfilled (a product launch and a reasonable follow-up window). Purchase records are kept for the period required by US tax law, typically seven years.
